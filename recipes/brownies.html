Lasagna

Description

This is the best freaking lasagna you will ever try. One bite and you'll be in food heaven. Trust me.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna sheets
  • Tinned tomatoes
  • Bechamel sauce
  • Hard cheese

Steps

  1. Layer up the ingredients on top of each other in a dish.
  2. Whack them in the oven until the cheese goes brown.
  3. Hey presto! Enjoy.
