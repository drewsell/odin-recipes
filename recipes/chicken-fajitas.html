Chicken fajitas

Description

These are the best freaking chicken fajitas you will ever try. One bite and you'll be in food heaven. Trust me.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Tortilla wraps
  • Peppers
  • Onions
  • Guacamole (optional)

Steps

  1. Cook the chicken, peppers, and onions in a pan.
  2. Heat the tortilla wraps.
  3. Add the chicken mixture to the tortilla wraps and fold. Add a slather of guacamole (optional).
  4. Enjoy!
